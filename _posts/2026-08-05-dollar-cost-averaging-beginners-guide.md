Dollar-cost averaging (DCA) means investing a fixed amount of money at
regular intervals, regardless of price.

## Why it works for many investors

Because the purchase amount stays constant, you naturally buy more shares
when prices are low and fewer when prices are high, which averages your
cost basis over time.

## Getting started

Most brokerages let you automate recurring purchases into an index fund or
ETF, which is the simplest way to put DCA into practice.
