Spot Bitcoin ETF flow data gets a lot of attention every trading day, but a
single day's inflow or outflow figure is a noisy signal on its own.

## What the number measures

Net flows represent the dollar value of ETF shares created minus shares
redeemed on a given day, which corresponds to new cash entering or leaving
the funds.

## Why context matters more than any one print

Multi-week trends, alongside price action and macro conditions, tend to be
far more informative than any single day's headline number.
